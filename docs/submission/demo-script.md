# Saath — 3-Minute Demo Script

Total: ~180 seconds. Narrator speaks; screen shows the app at
http://127.0.0.1:8091/web/ logged in as `demostudio`.
Record at 1280×800, dark mode on (the default).

## Opening line (say first, verbatim)
> "Everything you're about to see was built this week for this
> hackathon — there was no pre-existing Saath before the event."

## 0:00–0:20 — The promise (Home + dashboard)
> "Meet Saath — one companion for everything in daily life. It reminds,
> plans, teaches with video, and books — just tell it what you need."
- Show Home: Saath mascot, greeting, five entry points.
- Pan right: Your Day (real tasks), Daily check-in, Suggestions,
  Trusted connections — all live data, nothing hardcoded.
- Say "Hi Saath" out loud: Talk opens, hands-free. This is the
  independence moment — no tapping for elders or visually-impaired users.

## 0:20–0:50 — Real understanding → delegation (Talk)
> "I speak in plain words. Saath understands, plans, and its agents do
> the work — with my approval for anything real."
- Say or type: "Plan a trip to Jaipur for 3 relaxed days with good hotels."
- Show the plan; open My Life → Tasks: parent/child rows + audit events.
  This is the proof it is real orchestration, not a chat bubble.

## 0:50–1:15 — Bookings, honestly labeled (My Life → Bookings)
> "Saath walks through complete bookings — and never fakes a real one."
- Book a cab; point at the DEMO-XXXX reference and say the word DEMO.
- Approval popup appears; approve; show the stored booking.
- "Book this ride for real on Uber" → REAL HANDOFF panel, route filled
  in; the real transaction happens in Uber, honestly handed off.
- Flights tab: search BLR→DEL, show the test-mode banner, book through
  Approvals to a real Duffel test reference (no ticket, no charge).

## 1:15–1:45 — Visual Tutor video, generated locally (Learn → Videos)
> "And Saath teaches. This kettle tutorial was planned by Muse,
> illustrated by Muse, animated and narrated on this device."
- Play `How to use an electric kettle safely` (already COMPLETED).
- Point at the "Muse-powered illustrated video" label; show captions.
- Ask a follow-up: "Explain step two again."

## 1:45–2:15 — Real integrations (Calendar, Maps, Gmail)
> "Where Saath touches the real world, it touches it for real."
- Calendar: today's live Google events.
- Bookings → "Find real places": search "dentist", show the LIVE badge;
  "Get real route & time": distance, duration, turn-by-turn steps.
- Gmail: connected status; send-only scope stated honestly.

## 2:15–2:40 — Hindi + voice for everyone (Talk + Settings)
> "Saath speaks to everyone: Hindi replies and fully hands-free voice."
- Flip "Saath replies in" to Hindi; ask one question; show Hindi reply.
- Remind: every screen so far also works by voice alone.

## 2:40–3:00 — Trust close (Approvals)
> "And Saath never pretends: approvals gate every real action, failures
> are reported honestly, and 278 backend tests plus 53 voice checks
> guard it all."
- Flash the Approvals screen. End on Saath celebrating on Home.

## If something breaks on camera (say it, don't hide it)
- Muse quota exhausted → chat falls back to the honest offline stub.
  "That's the safety net working — Saath says what it is."
- Maps quota hit → "Google found nothing" honest empty state.
- Mic blocked → red "Mic blocked - tap to retry" pill; tap it once.

## Recording notes
- Start the server first (see checklist). Close personal tabs; use a
  fresh Chrome profile for the welcome overlay (optional first shot).
- Pre-open: Home, Talk, Bookings, Videos, Calendar.
- One blooper-free take; authenticity beats polish.
